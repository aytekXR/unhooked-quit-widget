App icon concepts

Four concepts from the creative inventory, built as literal compositions on the iOS squircle. All render on the Horizon Gradient — Harbor Teal #0C6F65 into Dusk Indigo #5262BC — the field reserved for the icon and marketing only. Each concept shows light / dark / tinted at 120px and a 29px proof row: an icon that dies at 29px dies in Settings. No red, no letterforms, no habit imagery, no people.

1 · Surfaced Breath — Refined RECOMMENDED — BUILD THIS

The existing crest geometry re-rendered with contemporary depth: crest enlarged ~8%, the hard 1px horizon replaced by a luminous waterline glow (Foam at 35% over the gradient seam, brightest at the crest's intersection), a whisper of vertical light falloff in the sky (top 15% brightened ≤6%), the submerged reflection deepened at 18% opacity, compressed to ~40% of crest height. Polysemous — sun rising, head above water, breath surfacing — habit-neutral, rename-proof, and it preserves recognition for existing TestFlight users.

Dark · field deepened toward #121417 @52% · crest stays luminous
Tinted · crest + waterline as one template on #1C1F24 — system applies the user's tint
Clear · crest on glass, shown over a wallpaper field
Composition grid: crest center at ~38% height · waterline at ~55% · reflection ellipse compressed to ~40% of crest height · waterline glow 4–6px soft-light band, Foam @35% over the seam. Assemble the full iOS 26 appearance set in Apple Icon Composer.
29px — Settings-row size. The glow reads where the old 1px hairline vanished.

2 · Waterline Keel PROTOTYPE AT 29PT FIRST

Above a thin Foam waterline, calm negative space in Harbor Teal; below, a single rounded keel-weight form in deep indigo hanging steady — the ballast itself. The only concept that literalizes the name without nautical cliché (anchors are banned territory — tattoo/navy connotations, and "stuck" contradicts forgiveness). Keel at 2:3 width-to-depth, centered mass just below the line so it reads held, not sinking.

Light · sky #0C6F65 · keel #0A5F57 → #5262BC · line Foam #FFFFFF
Dark · field deepened #121417 @50%
Tinted · keel + line as template
29px — the named risk: does the bottom-weighted form read as held or as sinking at glance distance?

3 · Breath Ring ARGUE AGAINST — KEEP ON FILE

A single soft teal ring, open at 12 o'clock, crest-dot resting in the gap, on a Dusk Indigo-to-near-black field — momentum ring and breath bloom collapsed into one mark. Ring stroke ≈ icon-width/11 · gap ≈ 28° · dot diameter ≈ 1.4× stroke. Risk: crowded territory (Activity rings, meditation apps); differentiation hangs entirely on the crest-dot surviving 29px.

Light · ring #4CC8B9 (dark-mode teal for luminosity) · field #5262BC → #121417
Dark · field deepened @35%
Tinted · ring + dot as template
29px — the crest-dot is the whole differentiation. Judge it here, not at 120.

4 · First Light BENCHMARK — DO NOT SHIP

No shapes: the two-zone horizon field alone with a soft light bloom breathing at the waterline — the moment before dawn. Maximally discreet (indistinguishable from a wallpaper app at shoulder distance — a genuine feature for this audience) and stunning in dark and tinted modes. Risk: near-invisible recall in App Library and search results. Keep as the ceiling of restraint the chosen icon is measured against.

Light · sky #0C6F65 · water #5262BC · bloom Foam #FFFFFF
Dark · night field #121417 @55% — the "11pm" register
Tinted · reduces to the waterline alone
29px — quiet to the point of anonymity. That is both the feature and the reason it stays a benchmark.

Discreet alternates — zero brand color, by definition

"Calendar style" and "Timer style" carry no Ballast color — the disguise is the absence of identity. Redrawn on the crest's geometric grid (same optical margins, same centering) so they feel like quality apps, not clip art. Grays only, no red anywhere: even the disguise obeys the palette law. Settings footer, verbatim: "The alternate icons carry no Ballast color, so your home screen stays quiet." Erase resets to the primary icon — an "erased" phone doesn't keep the disguise.

29px — the disguise must be boring at every size. A static date and a still clock: nothing to notice.
