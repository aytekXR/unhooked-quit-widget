Ballast Beta guide

Privacy Policy

Your streak lives on your phone.

Ballast has no account, no sign-up and no server holding your history. What you log stays on your device. This page says exactly what that means, and what the one optional exception is.

Last updated 2 August 2026.

The short version

What leaves your device, and when.

By default, nothing. There is no account to create, so there is nothing to link your data to. Your quits, your streaks, the notes you write, the times you log a slip and the answers you gave during onboarding are stored on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Analytics is optional and off until you turn it on. Onboarding asks once, the answer defaults to off, and you can change it later in Settings. If you leave it off, no analytics event is ever sent.

If you turn it on, Ballast sends a small set of product events — which steps of the app you reach, and the category of habit you are working on (for example "nicotine" or "alcohol"). That is deliberately coarse.

The line we hold

What is never sent, on any setting.

Even with analytics enabled, these never leave your device:

the notes and reasons you write; your free-text answers; the specific times you log anything; your streak history; any name you give a quit; and any identifier that would tie the above to you as a person.

Analytics events are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you across other apps or websites. Ballast contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers. Your information is not sold, and it is not shared for anyone else's marketing.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple.

If you subscribe, the payment is processed by Apple. Ballast never sees your card details. To know whether to unlock paid features, the app records whether a valid subscription exists — a purchase record, not a payment method.

Apple's own privacy terms cover the transaction itself, and your purchase history is available in your Apple Account settings.

Your control

Erase everything means everything.

Settings has an erase option that removes the app's data from your device — quits, streaks, notes, onboarding answers and the widget's copy of your progress. Deleting the app removes it too. Because there is no server-side copy, there is nothing left behind for anyone to return to you or to delete on request.

If you have analytics turned on and change your mind, turning it off in Settings stops any further events immediately.

Age and contact

Ballast is rated 17+.

The app is not directed to children, and it asks for your year of birth before anything else so that under-17 users are shown support resources rather than the app. That year is used for the check and is not stored.

Questions about any of this, including anything you would like removed, go to aytek@beyondkaira.com.
