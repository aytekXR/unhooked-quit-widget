The moment that matters

Help before you even unlock.

Most quit apps help you count days. The hard part is the ninety seconds when an urge arrives.

1

2

Breathe with the circle — in for 4, hold for 7, out for 8. Urges crest and pass, usually within about 15 minutes.

3

Then the app shows you your own reasons, in your own words, at the biggest type it has.

Forgiveness is a mechanic

A slip isn't day zero.

Log a slip and your best streak is archived, not erased. Your momentum score survives. Mistapped? Undo stays available for ten minutes. There's no red in this app, no countdowns, and no lecture — just the next hour, starting now.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Is this medical treatment?

No. Ballast tracks your progress and helps you through urges; it isn't medical care. If you drink heavily or daily, stopping suddenly can be physically risky — talk to a doctor. Free, confidential helplines are built into the app.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to create and nothing to log into.

What data do you collect?

Usage analytics are off unless you opt in — and even then, never your answers, notes, or the times you log. If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment and our billing provider receives your purchase history, which is what lets the app know you're subscribed. Nothing is used to track you across other apps, and there is no advertising identifier anywhere in the app.

What can I quit?

Vaping, alcohol, adult content, cannabis, doomscrolling, or anything you name yourself. "Cut down" is a valid goal, not a compromise.

What happens when I slip?

Your best streak is archived, your momentum survives, and you can undo within ten minutes. Never day zero.

Will people see it on my phone?

Only if you want them to. Discreet mode strips habit words from every widget, and the app icon can look like a calendar or timer.

If you need help right now

Ballast isn't a crisis service. If you're in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number, or find a free helpline in your country at findahelpline.com. The app carries verified lines for the regions it supports.
