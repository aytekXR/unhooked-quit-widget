Ballast Beta guide

Support

Something not working the way it should?

Ballast is made by one person, so there is no ticket queue and no bot — messages go straight to a human. Most things below have a two-minute answer, so it is worth a look first.

Email support

Written replies usually come within a couple of days. If you are testing a beta build, the fastest route is Share Beta Feedback from inside TestFlight — it attaches your device and build automatically.

Before you write

Three things that make a reply useful.

1

Your build number.

In the app: Settings, then scroll to the bottom. In TestFlight it is on the app's page.

2

Your device and iOS version.

iPhone model, and Settings › General › About › iOS Version.

3

What you expected instead.

A screenshot beats a description. Nothing you send is stored anywhere beyond the email itself.

The questions that come up most

Answers, shortest first.

The lock-screen widget shows nothing useful.

Open the app once. The widget reads a small feed that the app writes when it launches, so a freshly added widget has nothing to show until you have opened the app at least once after adding it. If you had a widget placed before an update, remove it and add Streak again — the widget changed kind, and a placed one from the older build simply disappears rather than updating in place.

How do I cancel, and who do I ask about a charge?

Apple handles all billing, so cancellation and refunds go through Apple rather than through us. On your iPhone: Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the renewal and you keep access until the period you already paid for ends. Refund requests are made through Apple's purchase history, and we cannot issue or refuse one on your behalf.

I paid, but the app still shows the subscription screen.

Tap Restore purchases on that screen. That asks Apple directly what your Apple Account owns and re-applies it, which also covers a reinstall or a new phone. If a purchase does not go through, the screen keeps a retry on it rather than sending you back to the start.

I got a new phone. Where did my streak go?

This is the honest cost of having no account. Everything lives on your device and there is no server of ours holding a copy, so your streak travels in a device backup — restoring a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings it along — but it will not appear by signing in somewhere, because there is nowhere to sign in. Setting a phone up as new starts the app fresh. Your subscription is separate and does follow your Apple Account.

Can I hide what I am tracking?

Yes, and it is built for that. Settings › Discreet Mode strips habit words from every widget so they show numbers only, the app icon can be changed to look like a calendar or a timer, and the app switcher shows a blank card instead of your screen. There is also a neutrally named Reset control that opens the same panic flow without naming anything.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Privacy & Data › Erase everything, then hold the button for about a second. It genuinely deletes — quits, notes, streaks, and the app icon resets — and there is no copy anywhere for us to remove on our end, because there was never one. Your subscription is not affected, since that belongs to your Apple Account.

The app will not install, or TestFlight will not offer it.

Ballast needs iOS 26 or newer. On an older version the build is not offered at all, which looks like a broken invite rather than a version mismatch. Check Settings › General › About and update if you can.

Does the app ever ask for permissions?

No — and not as a policy, but structurally. The app has no code that could raise a prompt for notifications, location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or health. If anything ever asks you for one, that is a bug and worth telling us about immediately.

If you are having a hard time

Ballast tracks your progress and helps you through urges; it isn't medical care, and it makes no clinical claims. Free, confidential helplines are built into the app under Settings › Support & resources, and they are reachable without a subscription. If you drink heavily or daily, stopping suddenly can be physically risky — both stopping and cutting down can need supervision, so it is worth talking to a doctor about the safest way forward. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
