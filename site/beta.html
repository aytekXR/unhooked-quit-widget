Ballast What is Ballast?

Thanks for testing Ballast.

This page is everything you need in one place: how to get the app, the one screen that looks broken and isn't, and the twenty minutes I'm asking for.

What it is

An app for cutting down or stopping a habit. You pick what you're working on, answer some questions, and it keeps your streak and holds on to the reasons you gave. There's a panic button for your lock screen for the moment an urge hits.

Getting in

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store. It's Apple's own app for trying betas.
  2. Open the email invite I sent you on your iPhone and tap the button in it. That's what links your Apple Account to the beta — the invite is per-person, so it needs to be your own.
  3. Install Ballast from TestFlight and open it.

Two things to check first

You need iOS 26 or newer. If TestFlight won't offer you the build, that's why — tell me and I'll sort it out.

Please turn on crash reports: Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers. Without it I can't see crashes, and that's most of what I need from a beta.

The one thing that will look broken and isn't

After the summary screen you'll hit a subscription screen with no close button. In TestFlight the purchase is free — Apple runs it as a test transaction and you are not charged. Please go through it, because it's the only way to see the rest of the app and it's one of the things I most need tested.

If you'd rather not, quit the app completely and reopen it, and you'll land on the main screen.

Your privacy, plainly

Nothing you enter leaves your phone. There's no account and no sign-in. The app doesn't ask for notifications, location, camera, or microphone — if anything ever asks you for a permission, that's a bug and I want to know immediately.

The twenty-minute pass

Roughly in this order. You don't have to be thorough — just honest.

  1. Install and open. Note anything slow or odd about the very first launch.
  2. The age check. A birth-year wheel. Enter your real year.
  3. The questions — 11 to 13 of them depending on your answers. Note roughly when you start and when the summary appears — that's a number I genuinely need. One question asks whether to share usage data; either answer is a valid test, so say what you'd actually say. Two involve typing a number — if you're outside the US or UK, watch the number keys closely and tell me whether what you type is what you see.
  4. The summary. Does it feel like it was written about you? Are your own words in it, spelled the way you typed them? Is the money figure plausible?
  5. The subscription screen. Read it before you tap. Price, trial length, what happens when the trial ends, the Terms and Privacy links, Restore — is anything missing or confusing? Then take the free trial.
  6. The widget moment. Follow it and add the "Streak" widget to your lock screen. Choose the wide rectangular one — it carries the panic button.
  7. Panic, from the lock screen. Lock the phone, then tap the panic button on the widget. It should go straight into a full-screen breathing exercise showing your reasons. Do the whole thing. How long from tap to something useful on screen? That's the single most important thing you can tell me.
  8. Log a slip. From the main screen. Read what comes back carefully — it should not make you feel bad. Then try undoing it.
  9. Settings. Go through every section: Panic access, Discreet Mode, Breathing, Privacy & Data, Support & resources. In Panic access, tap "Add the lock-screen widget" and confirm it re-opens the widget screen from step 6.
  10. Breathing → "Breathe with taps". Turn it on, then run the panic flow again with your eyes shut. Can you follow the rhythm by feel alone?

If one of these is you, there's a bit more

  • Vaping or nicotine. Answer the spend question honestly, then check the summary's yearly figure against your own arithmetic.
  • Adult content. Please run this one in Discreet Mode (below). The real question is whether you'd be comfortable with this app on your home screen with someone next to you. Say so plainly if not.
  • Alcohol. You should meet an amber "One thing worth knowing" card exactly once, on the summary. Tell me whether it lands as caring or as alarming. "Got it" should dismiss it forever, and it should never come back.
  • Cutting down rather than stopping. Pick a "cut down" goal — you get an extra step asking for a weekly limit. Check that the main screen respects it.

The discreet pass, if you're willing

  1. Settings → Discreet Mode → turn on the toggle for your streak. The widget should drop to numbers only, with no habit name anywhere on it.
  2. Settings → App Icon → "Calendar style" or "Timer style". The home-screen icon and its name change.
  3. Add the "Reset" control from the Control Center gallery instead of "Panic". Same flow, neutral name and glyph.
  4. The real test: hand your unlocked phone to someone. Can they tell what the app is for from the home screen, the widget, or the Control Center gallery?

Starting over

Settings → Privacy & Data → Erase everything, held for about a second. That genuinely deletes everything, resets the app icon, and puts the app back to a fresh install. Your subscription isn't affected — it lives with your Apple Account.

Known oddities — none of these are worth reporting

What you'll hitWhy
The subscription screen has no close button Expected for now. Take the free trial, or quit and reopen the app.
Your test subscription renews every day, then stops after about a week Apple's doing, not the app's. In TestFlight every plan renews once per 24 hours, up to six times. If you reopen the app around day 7 and something looks wrong, that is worth telling me.
Helplines only show real numbers for the US and Turkey Deliberate. Everywhere else gets written guidance and no phone number, because an unverified crisis number is worse than none.
The app never asks for notification permission Correct and deliberate. It has no way to ask.
The streak widget looks empty just after you add it Open the app once — that's what fills it. If it still looks wrong, please do tell me.
You had an older build and its widget vanished Remove and re-add "Streak" once. The widget changed underneath.

One that is worth reporting: if a price on the subscription screen disagrees with the price in Apple's own purchase sheet, tell me straight away.

How to tell me things

From inside TestFlight is easiest. Take a screenshot and tap Share Beta Feedback, or just shake the phone. It sends me the screenshot along with your device and build details, so I don't have to ask.

Wording matters as much as bugs here. If a sentence felt off, preachy, or confusing, that's exactly the kind of thing I want.

One ask

Please don't share the invite or post screenshots. The name isn't final yet, so the beta needs to stay off the public internet for now. Sending someone this page is fine — it has no invite in it.

If you need help right now

Ballast isn't a crisis service, and it isn't medical care. If you're in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number, or find a free helpline in your country at findahelpline.com.
