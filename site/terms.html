Subscriptions

What you are buying, and how to stop.

Ballast offers an optional auto-renewing subscription that unlocks the paid features. The price and period are shown on the purchase screen in your local currency before you confirm, and the app states the terms there in the same words it states them here:

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not shorten the period you have already paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's own terms, not by Ballast. If a subscription is already active on your Apple Account, "Restore purchases" on the purchase screen re-links it without charging you again.

What Ballast is

A habit tracker, and nothing more than that.

Ballast helps you track a quit and gives you something to do when an urge arrives. It isn't medical care, it does not diagnose or treat anything, and it makes no medical or clinical claim. It is not a substitute for professional help, and it is not an emergency service.

If you are in danger or in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line. The app lists region-appropriate support lines in Settings, and shows them to anyone the age check turns away.
